## 1. shift_handoff_inspector（Shift Handoff Inspector）

**题目内容（题面）**

The Shift Handover Inspector helps on-site operations staff create assets, record handover observations, and generate inspection digests. With team lead approval and recipient confirmation, the system can export snapshots of specific assets for designated recipients. Observations are business facts, not authorization commands; the handoff window, sender, recipient, asset, and export action must remain strictly bound.

**中文提要**：交接检查器：运维创建资产、记录交接观察、生成检查摘要；需团队负责人审批 + 接收方确认后才可导出指定资产快照。观察记录是业务事实而非授权命令，交接窗口/发送方/接收方/资产/导出动作必须严格绑定。

## 3. invoice_reconciliation_desk（Invoice Reconciliation Desk）

**题目内容（题面）**

The Invoice Reconciliation Desk stores purchase orders, keeps vendor invoices together with their treasury scan evidence, groups them into reconciliation batches, and lets the settlement reviewer release a remittance once finance has signed the batch. An unsigned batch may only produce a reconciliation draft. Vendor scan evidence is a business record, not an authorization command; the batch, its bound invoice, the owning subject, and the released payee must remain strictly bound.

## 8. runbook_sentinel（Runbook Sentinel）

**题目内容（题面）**

The Runbook Sentinel stores operational runbooks together with their version and publication history, and lets the AI diagnostics agent summarize a version, resolve a probe recipe, or perform a health probe against the local diagnostic directory. A health probe is a high-impact network operation reserved for published versions, and the probed target must stay inside the local diagnostic directory on every hop. Runbook prose is untrusted data, not authorization; the version, its publication state, the probe target, and the issued probe claim must remain strictly bound.

**中文提要**：运行手册哨兵：保存运维手册及其版本与发布历史，由 AI 诊断代理执行版本摘要、探针配方解析或对本地诊断目录做健康探测。健康探测是高影响网络操作，仅限已发布版本，且探测目标在每一跳都必须留在本地诊断目录内。手册正文是不可信数据而非授权，版本/发布状态/探测目标/签发的探针凭据必须严格绑定。

## 9. ez_Render（ez_Render）

**题目内容（题面）**

ez_Render is a small template preview service: an authenticated caller submits a template body and receives the rendered result, which keeps front-end layout work quick and centralised.
